Animight is the companion app for your custom Animight LED art panels — handcrafted illuminated artworks that transform any room into a living canvas.

EXPLORE CURATED DESIGNS
Browse an ever-growing gallery of curated lighting designs, each crafted specifically for Animight panels. From mesmerizing anime-inspired art to ambient mood scenes, discover designs that match your style and space.

PREVIEW BEFORE YOU APPLY
Tap any design to enter a full-screen immersive preview. See the color palette, LED-readiness status, and a simulated glow effect showing exactly how the design will look on your physical panel — before you connect.

CONNECT INSTANTLY VIA BLUETOOTH
Pair with your Animight panel hardware in seconds over Bluetooth Low Energy. No complicated setup, no Wi-Fi passwords — just tap, connect, and your panel lights up with your chosen design.

MOOD LIGHTING PRESETS
Go beyond static designs with built-in mood presets: Chill Vibes, Party Mode, Focus, Sunset, Aurora, and Gaming. Each preset sends a unique combination of colors and animation patterns to your panel, instantly transforming the atmosphere of your room.

FULL CONTROL AT YOUR FINGERTIPS
Adjust brightness, toggle loop animations, activate sound-reactive mode, or power off — all from the sleek control dashboard. Real-time Bluetooth communication means zero lag between your tap and the panel response.

BUILD YOUR COLLECTION
Save your favorite designs to your personal collection for instant access. Reconnect to previously paired panels with a single tap.

CLOUD-POWERED & ALWAYS FRESH
New designs are added regularly to the cloud gallery. Your library is always up to date, with no app updates required to see the latest creations.
